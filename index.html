About Me

I'm Ariane Lesnyak, a localization professional who has spent years on both sides of the workflow: translating and subtitling content, and coordinating the people, deadlines and review cycles that get it delivered.

I've subtitled, reviewed and QC'd audiovisual content, led content production with 15+ writers and editors, and built small tools to make repetitive quality work faster and more reliable.

Now I want to bring that end-to-end view to project management at VSI.
