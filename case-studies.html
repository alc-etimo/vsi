TeamWorker / Claro · Internal content production

Project Coordination:
From Brief to Delivery

Different requesters. Multiple contributors. One accountable process. At TeamWorker/Claro, I coordinated content requests from the first briefing through assignment, follow-up, revision and approval.

Production workflowIllustrative interface · fictional sample request

Checkpoint

Illustrative schedule risk: confirm availability, rebalance assignments and agree a revised delivery date. Production and review depend on the updated schedule.

The challenge

Requests came from different business areas. Requirements, responsibilities and deadlines needed to be clear before work moved between contributors.

My responsibility

I clarified briefs, assigned demands, answered questions, followed up on delivery and redistributed work when needed. I conducted review, correction and approval cycles.

Transferable to localization

Brief preparation, resource allocation and quality gates apply across production environments. This reflects internal content coordination, not an executed marketing campaign or management of dubbing vendors.
